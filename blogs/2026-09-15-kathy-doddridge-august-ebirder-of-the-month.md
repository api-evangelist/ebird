Please join us in congratulating Kathy Doddridge of Bradenton, Florida—winner of the August 2026 eBirder of the Month Challenge, sponsored by ZEISS. Kathy’s name was randomly selected from the 9500 eBirders who submitted 31 or more eligible checklists in August.
