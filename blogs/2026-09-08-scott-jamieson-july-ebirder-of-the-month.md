Please join us in congratulating Scott Jamieson of Melbourne, Australia—winner of the July 2026 eBirder of the Month Challenge, sponsored by ZEISS. Scott’s name was randomly selected from the 3746 eBirders who submitted 20 or more eligible checklists from a single location in July.
