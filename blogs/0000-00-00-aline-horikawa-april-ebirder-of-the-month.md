Please join us in congratulating Aline Horikawa of Otsu, Shiga, Japan—winner of the April 2026 eBirder of the Month Challenge, sponsored by ZEISS. Aline’s name was randomly selected from the 518 eBirders who submitted 5 or more eligible eBird checklists, each containing at least one 1-10 minute soundscape recording.
