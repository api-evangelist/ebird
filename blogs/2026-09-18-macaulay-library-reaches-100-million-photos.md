Since 2015, over 333,000 photographers around the world have contributed their photos to eBird checklists, growing the Macaulay Library into the world’s largest archive of natural history media. This week, we passed a major milestone: thanks to the collective effort of eBirders, the Macaulay Library now contains over 100 million photos!
