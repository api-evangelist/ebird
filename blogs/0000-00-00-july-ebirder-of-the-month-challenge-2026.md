The July eBirder of the Month challenge, sponsored by ZEISS, will help you get to know the birds in one place—whether that's your home, favorite park, or another location you visit frequently. The eBirder of the Month will be drawn from eBirders who submit 20 or more eligible checklists from one location in July.
